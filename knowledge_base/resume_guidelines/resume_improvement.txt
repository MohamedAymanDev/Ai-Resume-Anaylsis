Resume Improvement Guidelines

A strong technical resume should contain:

1. Professional summary
The summary should clearly describe the candidate's technical background and target role.

2. Technical skills
List relevant programming languages, frameworks, libraries, databases, and tools.

3. Projects
Projects should describe the problem, technologies used, and measurable results when available.

4. Experience
Describe responsibilities and achievements using concise bullet points.

5. Education
Include degree, university, field of study, and graduation year.

6. Certifications
Include relevant technical certifications and training programs.

7. Project descriptions
Use action-oriented descriptions and focus on technical contributions and outcomes.

Avoid:
- Unnecessary personal information
- Very long paragraphs
- Unsupported claims
- Irrelevant skills
- Excessive formatting
